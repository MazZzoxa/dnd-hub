# D&D Hub v0.5.1 — LAN Campaign Roles

Patch release for the v0.5 LAN multiplayer layer.

## Added

- persistent local `client_id`;
- automatic Campaign Member creation when a Player joins;
- `campaign_members.client_id` identity binding;
- separate Owned / Joined campaign lists;
- GM Campaign interface and Player read-only Campaign interface;
- Player character linking from the player's own device;
- server-side role permissions for Player mutations.

## Behavior

- GM starts the temporary FastAPI server and is not counted as a Player.
- Players who join are automatically represented as Campaign Members on the GM side.
- A Player can change only their linked character and its child data.
- Campaign metadata, members and sessions remain GM-controlled.

## Scope

LAN/WebSocket remains the only active transport. Wi-Fi P2P, Bluetooth and Internet/VPN transport stay deferred.
